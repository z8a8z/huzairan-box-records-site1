Music Producer & Sound Architect

“a pro boombab soul producer, that loves what he do”

Founded in 2020 right on Huzairan Street in Kirkuk, Iraq, huzairan box records was born out of an uncompromising mission: to bring the golden era of hip-hop and soul back to the streets with pure authenticity and zero shortcuts.

While the industry drifted toward synthetic soundscapes, lead producer abd alaziz abdullah committed his craft to dusty vinyl grooves, SP-1200 style bounce, rich Rhodes chords, soulful vocal chops, and the unmistakable head-nod rhythm of true boom-bap.

Under client leadership and studio coordination by aziz, our studio provides local and international emcees, vocalists, and creators with an inspiring sanctuary. Every session is tracked with meticulous acoustics, analog saturation, and deep artistic respect.

STUDIO CAPABILITIES

Services & Production Suite

Complete analog and digital recording solutions engineered for authentic commercial releases.

01 / PRODUCTION

Boom-Bap Beat Production

Signature drum programming with authentic swing, rare soul crate sampling, custom basslines, and punchy transients designed for true lyricism.

02 / RECORDING

Studio Vocal Tracking

Pristine vocal capture in our dedicated acoustically isolated booth. High-grade condenser microphones and vintage outboard preamps for maximum warmth.

03 / MIXING & MASTERING

Analog Mixing & Tape Master

Summing through analog hardware, tube harmonic saturation, tape emulation, and surgical EQ balance for loud, dynamic, commercial-ready masters.

04 / CURATION

Soul Sampling & Crate Digging

Decades of vinyl archaeology brought into modern arrangements. Key matching, harmonic pitch manipulation, and texture design.

05 / ARTIST DEVELOPMENT

Session Direction & Arrangement

Hands-on producer guidance by abd alaziz abdullah on cadence, hook structure, double tracking, and full track cohesion.

06 / DISTRIBUTION

Global Release Delivery

Assistance with digital streaming platform delivery (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube), metadata curation, and cassette/vinyl runs.
